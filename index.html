About

README.md

I've been at Amazon since 2022, where my work spans two layers. On the backend, I move high-volume systems onto event-driven architecture, most recently a seller compliance scoring system that now makes enforcement decisions in minutes instead of hours.

Closer to customers, I build AI-powered features: a search widget that hands conversational queries to an AI assistant, retrieval sources for an LLM ads system, and recommendations on voice-assistant smart displays. I also wrote an MCP server of agent scripts that cut our on-call troubleshooting time by two-thirds.

Before Amazon I did support engineering at L-Soft and integration work at fusionSpan. I studied Computer Science and Mathematics at the University of Maryland.
